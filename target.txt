Technology has become an integral part of modern society, shaping the way people interact, work, and live their daily lives. From the early days of basic computing to the era of artificial intelligence and automation, technological advancements have continuously transformed industries and lifestyles. The internet, for example, has revolutionized communication, enabling instant connectivity across the globe through emails, video calls, and social media platforms. Smartphones have further enhanced accessibility, allowing users to stay connected, access information, and perform tasks from virtually anywhere. In the healthcare sector, technology has led to groundbreaking innovations such as robotic surgeries, telemedicine, and AI-assisted diagnostics, improving patient outcomes and making medical services more efficient. Similarly, education has seen a shift toward digital learning, with online courses, interactive e-books, and virtual classrooms making knowledge more accessible to students worldwide.
